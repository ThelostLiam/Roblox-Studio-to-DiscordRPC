How to setup "RobloxStudio to DiscordRPC"

1. Go to https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/135394870049325/Studio-Status-Syncer
2. Install the plugin to the studio, it wont appear on the "plugin" tab in your studio, dont worry! Its installed and runs on Roblox Studio by default
3. Go to https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/135394870049325/Studio-Status-Syncer
4. Download the ZIP file
5. Unzip the Downloaded ZIP and run the RobloxStudio to DiscordRPC.exe
6. Make sure to keep Discord and Roblox Studio opened in the background
7. Once the program communicates with the Roblox's plugin you just installed, the CMD will disapper and can be opened from the hidden icons at the taskbar.
8. Open your Roblox Studio's Experience Settings and Turn on "HTTP Request" to work flawlessly!
9. Enjoy and flex!

Roblox Plugin: https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/135394870049325/Studio-Status-Syncer
